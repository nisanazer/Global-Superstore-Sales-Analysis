# Global Superstore Sales Analysis

An interactive Power BI dashboard that summarizes Global Superstore sales, profit, orders, and quantity. It includes a KPI overview, geographic and market comparisons, shipping mode distribution, and transaction details.

## Dashboard Overview

The report contains two views:

- **Sales Dashboard** — KPI cards, shipping mode distribution, and sales breakdowns by city, state, region, and market.
- **Transaction Details** — a detailed transaction table with supporting summaries by shipping mode, city, state, region, and market.

Country, region, and market filters are available on the dashboard.

## Key Insights

- **$12.64M in total sales** and **$1.47M in total profit** are shown in the KPI summary.
- The report records **25.035K orders** and **178K units sold**.
- **Central** is the leading region by sales at approximately **$2.8M**, followed by **South** at **$1.6M**.
- **APAC** is the highest-selling market at approximately **$3.6M**, followed by **EU** at **$2.9M** and **US** at **$2.3M**.
- **New York City** leads the displayed city ranking at approximately **$0.26M** in sales.
- **England** leads the displayed state ranking at approximately **$0.49M** in sales.
- **Standard Class** accounts for the largest share of orders, at approximately **59.6%**. Same Day has the smallest share, at approximately **5.3%**.

Figures are reported as displayed in the dashboard and may change when filters are applied.

## Files

## Open the Dashboard

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open `nisa.pbix` in Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. Use the dashboard filters and visuals to explore the data.

## Data and Refresh

The report contains transaction-level details and summary views. Refreshing the report may require access to its configured data source.
